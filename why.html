Our Why

Scroll to explore

We're a new milk company that is on a mission to bring delicious, high-quality, and affordable alt-milk to Asia. We believe that dairy milk needs an upgrade, and that's why we are committed to providing a plant-based alternative that is not only better for your stomach but also better for the planet. So go nuts for Dehusk, and make the switch to a healthier, more sustainable, and delicious alternative to dairy milk!

Here's why we do what we do

More than 80% of Asians are lactose intolerant.

We specialize in plant-based drinks free from lactose, soy, nuts, or gluten allergens. Our commitment to creating better alternatives comes from our understanding that 80% of Asians are lactose intolerant. We want to ensure that everyone can enjoy a refreshing drink without worry.

South East Asia is extremely reliant on dairy imports.

The Philippines alone imports 99% of their dairy supply. One day we'll be supporting the whole world, but right now we're focused on home – South East Asia. We use all things local, and we aim to support the local farmers and businesses.

Tasty doesn't have to be unhealthy.

With micronutrient deficiency an issue among our communities, we wanna make a treat without compromise. Energy boost with every sip? Say less.

Dairy creates 2-5x more CO2 than plant-based milk.

This statistic truly speaks for itself.
